Yes — but you also need a valid International Driving Permit (IDP). Under our current rental document requirements, an India-issued driving licence alone is not sufficient.

Why Do You Need Both an Indian Licence and an IDP?

They are two different documents. Your Indian licence remains your national driving licence; the IDP is an additional international permit used with it when required.

Your Indian licence remains essential

An International Driving Permit does not replace your national driving licence. If an IDP is required, both documents should be carried together.

FAQ for Indian Driving Licence Holders

Short answers to the most relevant questions for this page.

Do I need an IDP with an Indian driving licence in Dubai?

Yes under our current rental document requirements. Bring your valid Indian driving licence together with a valid International Driving Permit.

Can I rent a car in Dubai with only my Indian driving licence?

Not under the current requirement used for this page. An India-issued driving licence should be accompanied by a valid IDP.

Is an International Driving Permit the same as my Indian driving licence?

No. Your Indian driving licence is your national licence. The IDP is a separate international document used with it when required.

What if my passport was issued by another country but my driving licence is Indian?

Use the main IDP checker to verify your exact passport and driving licence combination.

Which documents should I prepare before collecting the car?

Prepare your passport, valid Indian driving licence and valid IDP. For the complete rental checklist, see documents required to rent a car in the UAE.
