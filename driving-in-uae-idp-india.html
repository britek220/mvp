Can You Drive in Dubai with an Indian Driving Licence?

Yes — if you also have a valid International Driving Permit (IDP). Under our current rental document requirements, an India-issued driving licence alone is not sufficient.

✓

Quick answer: Yes, with a valid IDP.

Bring your original valid Indian driving licence and your original valid International Driving Permit. The IDP is an additional document and does not replace your Indian licence.

Why Is an IDP Needed with an Indian Driving Licence?

The main point for customers is simple: the IDP is a separate supporting driving document used together with your national licence when required.

Your Indian licence is still your primary driving licence

The International Driving Permit does not turn into or replace your Indian licence. When an IDP is required, you should carry both documents together.

FAQ for Indian Driving Licence Holders

Short answers to the questions most relevant to this country page.

Do I need an IDP with an Indian driving licence in Dubai?

Yes under our current rental document requirements. Bring your original valid Indian driving licence and a valid original International Driving Permit.

Can I rent a car in Dubai with only my Indian driving licence?

Not under the current requirement used for this page. An India-issued driving licence should be accompanied by a valid IDP.

Is an International Driving Permit the same as my Indian driving licence?

No. They are separate documents. Your Indian licence is the national driving licence; the IDP is an additional international permit.

What if my passport was issued by another country but my driving licence is Indian?

Use the main IDP checker to check the exact passport and driving licence combination.

What documents should I prepare before collecting the car?

Prepare your original passport, original valid Indian driving licence and original valid IDP. For the full checklist, see documents required to rent a car in the UAE.
