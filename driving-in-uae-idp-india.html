Can You Drive in Dubai with an Indian Driving Licence?

If your driving licence was issued in India, our current rental-document matrix requires a valid International Driving Permit (IDP) in addition to your original Indian driving licence.

✓

Quick answer: Yes — you need an IDP.

Bring both your original valid Indian driving licence and your original valid International Driving Permit. The IDP does not replace your Indian licence.

FAQ for Indian driving licence holders

This section should later be expanded with Search Console queries, keyword research and recurring questions from support.

Do I need an IDP with an Indian driving licence in Dubai?

For the current rental-document matrix used by this prototype, yes. Bring your original Indian driving licence and a valid original IDP.

Can I rent a car in Dubai with only my Indian driving licence?

Not under the current requirement used for this page. The page should direct Indian licence holders to prepare both the national licence and IDP.

Is an International Driving Permit the same as my Indian driving licence?

No. They are separate documents. Your Indian licence is the national licence; the IDP is an additional international permit used with it where required.

What if my passport was issued by another country but my driving licence is Indian?

The production page should check your exact passport × India-issued licence combination using the same central requirements engine as the main IDP checker.

Can I use a digital copy of my IDP?

Do not rely on a digital copy as your only document. The page should tell customers to bring the original required documents unless the business rules explicitly state otherwise.
