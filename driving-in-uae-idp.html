International Driving Permit FAQ

Use this section for broad IDP queries while country-specific questions live on country landing pages.

What is an International Driving Permit?

An IDP is a separate international driving document used alongside a valid national driving licence where it is required. It should not be presented as a replacement for the national licence.

Do all tourists need an IDP in Dubai?

No single answer should be hardcoded for every tourist. The requirement depends on the relevant passport and driving-licence combination in the current requirements matrix.

Can I use only my IDP without my national driving licence?

No. The page should clearly explain that the IDP is used together with the original valid national driving licence.

What if the checker cannot confirm my combination?

The correct UX is to show “Unknown” and ask the customer to contact support before booking, rather than making a guessed Yes/No decision.

Can I share my checker result with someone?

In production, the selected passport and driving licence can be stored in query parameters so a support agent can send a prefilled link. Those parameter URLs should not become separate SEO pages.
