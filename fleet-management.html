Ownership & operating structure

You own the fleet — we run the operation

In the UAE, rental vehicles must operate through a licensed car rental company. The right structure therefore depends on the size of your investment.

Investments below $200,000

For one vehicle or a small fleet, setting up a separate company and licence is usually not practical. The vehicle is placed under a management agreement with Rentico and registered within our licensed rental structure.

Investments from $200,000

For investors building a fleet of around 10 vehicles or more, we can help establish a dedicated legal entity and obtain a car rental licence. The fleet operates through the investor’s own structure, while Rentico manages day-to-day operations.

Day-to-day operations

Rentico handles sales, marketing, bookings, customer service, vehicle maintenance, utilization and the daily rental operation.

Reporting & payouts

You receive clear reporting on revenue, costs and fleet performance, with payouts made according to the agreed management structure.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I start with one vehicle?

Yes. For a small number of vehicles, the car is placed under a management agreement with Rentico and operated within our licensed rental structure.

How is the vehicle set up for rental?

In the UAE, a rental vehicle must operate through a licensed car rental company. For smaller investments, the vehicle is placed under a management agreement with Rentico and registered within our licensed structure. From $200,000, we can help investors establish a separate legal entity and obtain their own licence.

How do payouts work?

The payout schedule and terms are defined in the management agreement. You also receive regular reporting on vehicle performance.

What happens if the vehicle is damaged?

The vehicle’s condition is documented at handover and return. Any incident follows a defined documentation and insurance process.

Can I take my vehicle back?

Yes. The notice period and exit terms are defined in the management agreement in advance.
