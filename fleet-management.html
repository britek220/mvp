FAQ

Clear answers before you start.

Who owns the vehicle?+

You remain the legal owner. The management agreement defines Rentico’s operational responsibilities.

Can I start with one vehicle?+

Yes. The service is designed for owners of one vehicle as well as small and larger fleets.

How are owner payouts calculated?+

The exact model will be defined in the agreement. Reporting should clearly show revenue, agreed costs and the owner payout.

What happens if the vehicle is damaged?+

Each incident follows a documented inspection, reporting and insurance coordination process.

Who coordinates maintenance?+

Rentico coordinates scheduled service, cleaning, inspections and approved repairs as part of fleet operations.

Can I withdraw the vehicle?+

Withdrawal terms and notice periods are defined transparently in the fleet management agreement.
