02 · Licence + IDP

Why both documents matter

The IDP does not replace your Indian driving licence. They are separate documents and should be carried together when an IDP is required.

In practical terms

Your Indian licence is still your national driving licence. The International Driving Permit is an additional international document used alongside it.

07 · FAQ

FAQ for Indian licence holders

Do I need an IDP with an Indian driving licence in Dubai?

Yes under our current rental document requirements. Carry your valid Indian driving licence together with a valid International Driving Permit.

Can I rent a car in Dubai with only my Indian driving licence?

Not under the current requirement used for this page. An India-issued driving licence should be accompanied by a valid IDP.

Is an IDP the same as my Indian driving licence?

No. Your Indian driving licence is your national licence. The IDP is a separate international document used with it when required.

What if my passport was issued by another country?

Use the main IDP checker to verify the exact passport and driving licence combination.

Which documents should I prepare before collecting the car?

Prepare your passport, valid Indian driving licence and valid IDP. For the complete checklist, see documents required to rent a car in the UAE.
