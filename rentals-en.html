FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is there a minimum fleet size?

You can apply with a fleet of any size. The Rentico team will review your vehicles, categories and rental terms and suggest a suitable listing format.

Who sets the prices and rental terms?

Prices and rental terms are agreed with your company before listing. You stay in control of rental rates, availability, mileage, delivery and additional services.

What do I need to list my cars?

To get started, we need your company and vehicle details, current photos, prices and key rental terms. The Rentico team will guide you through the remaining steps.

Do I need a technical integration?

Not necessarily at the beginning. We agree on how availability and bookings will be shared after your application, so you can start with a simple workflow.

How will I receive bookings?

The Rentico team will agree with you on a convenient communication channel and booking confirmation process. The workflow can later be expanded or automated if needed.

How much does it cost to list on Rentico?

Commercial terms depend on the working model and are agreed before launch. Send your details and the Rentico team will explain the terms for your fleet.

What types of cars can I list?

Economy, Standard, SUV, Business, Sport, Luxury and other in-demand categories. Vehicles should match their description, be roadworthy and ready for rental.

How quickly can I get started?

Timing depends on how quickly fleet information and terms are finalized. The more complete your application is, the sooner the Rentico team can prepare your cars for listing.
